# Worker Guide

Read this guide once when receiving a Task. Check your scope, settled contract, and completion conditions before starting.

- Within the assigned contract and scope, decide implementation details, local design, and failure diagnosis autonomously. Do not ask the parent about routine small decisions.
- Contact the parent when work is complete, when an unresolved decision blocks progress, or when requirements or scope must change. Avoid periodic updates, parameter confirmations, and granular edit reports.
- When blocked, report the facts, what you tried, and the decision needed. Continue independent work while waiting.
- Research may cover a broad area when the goal calls for it. Summarize facts, source locations, hypotheses, and unverified scope concisely; do not paste large amounts of source or logs.
- Run only the requested checks and stop once required normal behavior is confirmed. Do not add exhaustive checks, precautionary reruns, or unrelated improvements.
- Preserve existing changes and other workers' scope. Do not silently edit files outside your assignment.
- Keep the Report current with the final state and result. Brief messages are fine; do not repeat the same information to the parent.
- Write Task and Report content in English by default. Keep user-facing dialogue and Q&A in Japanese.
